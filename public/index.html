The problem

Scams happen on the call. That's where protection is missing.

Why people lose money

  • ✕Number blockers miss new numbers. Scammers change them every day.
  • ✕Once the call is answered, nothing protects the person.
  • ✕Scammers pressure and isolate: "act now", "don't hang up", "tell no one".
  • ✕Codes and bank transfers happen in minutes, before family can step in.

How Wessuwan protects

  • ✓Checks every caller sentence the moment it ends.
  • ✓Warns the person inside the call, with a calm voice message.
  • ✓Warns instantly when a caller asks for a code.
  • ✓Alerts the family by SMS with a one-tap way to call.
  • ✓Deletes normal calls when they end. Audio is never stored.

How it works

Four steps, all during the call

  1. 📢
    STEP 1

    The caller hears a notice

    Calls from unknown numbers hear that the line is protected by AI before they're connected.

  2. 🧠
    STEP 2

    Each sentence is checked by Jev

    Speech becomes text live, and Jev scores every caller sentence for urgency, threats and requests for codes or money.

  3. 🔊
    STEP 3

    A warning plays in the call

    At high risk, the protected person hears a calm warning: don't send money, don't share codes, hang up.

  4. 📱
    STEP 4

    The family gets an SMS

    Family members get a text saying who the caller claims to be, with a one-tap way to call.
