Privacy notice

We can't leak what we don't keep.

This notice uses GDPR-style wording and covers the rights you have under data protection law.

What we check

Wessuwan protects one person from phone scams. Calls from trusted contacts are connected straight through and never processed. Every other caller first hears: "This call is protected by Wessuwan, an AI scam protection service." Only after this announcement has finished is the conversation turned into text and checked by an AI for four warning signs: urgency, threats, requests for codes or personal data, and requests for money.

What we keep, and for how long

DataWhat happens to it
Call audioNever stored. It passes through memory to the speech-to-text service and is gone.
Normal call transcriptDeleted the moment the call ends.
Scam call transcriptDeleted the moment the call ends.
Scam summary (date, caller number, scam type, risk, one-sentence reason, alerts sent)Encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and deleted automatically after 30 days.
Your settings and consent recordEncrypted, kept until you change or delete them.
Server logsPhone numbers are masked (e.g. +1***-***-1234). Conversation text is never logged in production.

Real users are only protected with paid AI tiers set up for no training and minimal data retention. Free tiers, which may use data for training, are only used with test data.

Consent

Protection only starts after the protected person (or their legal representative) agrees on the setup page; we record the date and their name. You can withdraw at any time by turning protection off or deleting your data. Callers are informed by the announcement on every call, in every country.

Your rights and how to delete your data

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and a copy of your data, and complain to your data protection authority.

Delete everything yourself: open Setup → Delete all my data, type DELETE and confirm. All summaries, trusted contacts, family numbers and the consent record are removed and protection is switched off. To pause instead, use Turn protection off.
